# Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL) Framework

The Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL) Framework is a ready-to-use set of outcomes, indicators and methodologies that lets a MEL team with a limited budget conduct comprehensive, expert-informed evaluations of solutions. It is packaged as a digital tool to reduce the cost of doing science-led impact assessment. There are two parts to the tool:

1. The **MEL Plan**, which helps determine what outcomes are to be evaluated and the associated indicators to be measured.
2. The **MEL Dashboard**, which helps with digital data collection, real-time visualisation and analytics, and impact reporting.

Together, they remove the need for a MEL team to design the MEL exercise independently or consult specialists such as hydrologists and sociologists, both of which can be time-consuming and expensive.
